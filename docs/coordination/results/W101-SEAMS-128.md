Native support checks now merge exact shared XYZ edges only when exactly two
triangles are coplanar and form a strictly convex quadrilateral. The external
boundary is footprint-inset; the internal diagonal is not. Height and slope
checks still apply. Nonmanifold edges, gaps, mismatched heights and concave pairs
do not merge. This is deliberately narrower than arbitrary polygon union.

spheres, cylinders and tubes under the existing transform convention. Full
dimensions and length-plus-radius bounds deliberately over-enclose uncertain
half-length conventions. These are conservative rejection bounds, not exact
OBB/triangle clearance and not verified role classifications. Planes/rays remain
unsupported. All observed nonmesh primitives in the three saved zones receive
bounds: Commons417, Pavilion114, Olde Town276. Runtime coverage remains false.
